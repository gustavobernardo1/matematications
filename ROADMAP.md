# Study directions

This is a flexible study plan, not a quota or a claim of completed work.

- Discrete state updates, conservation, conditioning, numerical methods and pure mathematical explorations.
- Linear and convex optimization, battery dispatch, nonlinear and stochastic optimization.
- Monte Carlo, sensitivity, uncertainty propagation and probabilistic prediction/calibration.
- Physical baselines and parameter identification; classical regression and residual learning with held-out data.
- Automatic differentiation, differentiable simulation, neural ODEs, neural operators and data assimilation when justified.
- Selected paper reproductions and mathematical visualizations.

Prioritize independent understanding and quality. Link to helios and other applications without duplicating code; conduct comparative benchmark research in metron. The former scientific-ml study directions are now integrated here.
